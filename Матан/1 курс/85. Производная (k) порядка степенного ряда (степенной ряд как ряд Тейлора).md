
---
## выводим формулу:
$$\Large
b_0 + \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} b_{n}(x-a)^n = S(x) \qquad I, \qquad R>0
$$
$$\Large
b'_0 + \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} b_{n}((x-a)^n)' = S'(x) \qquad I, \qquad R
$$
$$\Large
\dots
$$
$$\Large
k\ge 2: \quad \qquad b^{(k)}_0 + \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} b_n ((x-a)^n)^{(k)} = S^{(k)}(x)\qquad I, \qquad R
$$
$$\Large
(\text{верно при $\forall x \in I$})
$$
---
$$\Large
\text{Рассмотрим } \ ((x-a)^n)^{(k)} :
$$
1.
$$\Large
k<n
$$
$$\Large
((x-a)^n)' = n(x-a)^{n-1}
$$
$$\Large
((x-a)^n)'' = n(n-1)(x-a)^{n-2}
$$
$$\Large
\dots
$$
$$\Large
((x-a)^n)^{(k)} = n(n-1)\dots(n-k+1) \cdot (x-a)^{n-k}
$$
2.
$$\Large
n=k
$$
$$\Large
((x-a)^n)^{(n)} = n!
$$
3.
$$\Large
n<k
$$
$$\Large
((x-a)^n)^{(k)} \equiv 0
$$
---
$$\Large
\left. ((x-a)^n)^{(k)}\right|_{x = a} = \begin{cases}  
0,\ k \ne n \\  
n!,\ n = k
\end{cases}
$$
$$\Large
\left. S^{(k)}(a) = b_0^{(k)} + \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} b_n ((x-a)^n)^{(k)} \right|_{x = a}, \quad k\ge 1
$$
$$\Large
S^{(k)}(a) = b_k \cdot k!
$$
$$\Large
\text{Получаем: } \quad b_k = \frac{S^{(k)}(a)}{k!}
$$
$$\Large
b_0 = S(a)
$$
$$\Large
\Rightarrow \ S(x) = S(a) + \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{S^{(n)}(a)}{n!} (x-a)^n
$$
